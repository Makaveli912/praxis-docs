INTRO

Introduction

Praxis is a binary (YES/NO) prediction market protocol deployed as a Go plugin (Nested Chain) on the Canopy Network. Markets are created by admins, predictions are submitted by any address, and outcomes are determined by a decentralized network of staked resolvers.

All state is stored on-chain. All transactions are signed with BLS12-381 G2 keys. The protocol enforces conflict-of-interest guards, position caps, and resolver reputation scoring.

$PRX is the native token of the Praxis protocol. All fees, bonds, stakes, and rewards are denominated in uPRX (1 PRX = 1,000,000 uPRX).

1.2 Submit Prediction

Any address can buy YES or NO shares in an open market. Cost is determined by the ADLMSR (Adaptive Dynamic Logarithmic Market Scoring Rule) pricing formula.

Cost formula: cost = B × ln(e^(qYES/B) + e^(qNO/B)) delta — where B is the liquidity parameter and q values are current pool sizes.
  • Market must be STATUS_OPEN and current block must be before ExpiryTime
  • Shares must be ≥ 1 (minimum 1 PRX)
  • A 2% market fee is applied on top of the trade cost
  • Max cost (slippage protection) — if actual cost exceeds MaxCost, TX is rejected
  • COI-3: A single address cannot hold more than 20% of the total pool value
  • TX fee: 10,000 uPRX split 50% fee pool / 50% treasury
1.3 Claim Winnings

After a market is FINALIZED or CANCELLED, bettors can claim their payout or refund.

  • FINALIZED: payout = (claimant shares / total winning shares) × losing pool, minus 2% treasury cut
  • CANCELLED: refund = full CostPaid amount returned to bettor account
  • Cannot claim twice — position is deleted after successful claim
  • Claim window: must claim within 1,000 blocks after finalization
1.4 / 1.5 Reclaim Stake / Cancel Market

Reclaim Stake — allows a bettor to reclaim their stake from a voided or error-state market.

Cancel Market — the market creator can cancel their market before expiry, provided no predictions have been submitted.

  • Only callable by the market creator
  • Market must be STATUS_OPEN and not yet expired
  • TotalPositions must be 0 — cannot cancel if any bettor has submitted a prediction
  • On cancel: CreatorBond (5,000 PRX) + LockedReserve (50 PRX) returned to creator
  • Open market counter decremented (spam cap relief)
02

Resolver

Resolvers are staked participants who propose outcomes for expired markets. They earn rewards proportional to their RRS (Resolver Reputation Score) and resolution history.

2.1 Register as Resolver

Registers a new resolver or adds stake to an existing registration. Resolver addresses are indexed on-chain at state prefix 0x2A.

TierRRS RangePanel Weight
🥉 Bronze10 – 491×
🥈 Silver50 – 992×
🥇 Gold100+3×
  • New registration: stake ≥ MIN_RESOLVER_STAKE (500,000 PRX)
  • Re-registration: total stake after addition must be ≥ MIN_RESOLVER_STAKE
  • RRS restored to minimum (10) if degraded on re-registration after full exit
  • Cannot register if UnbondingAmount > 0 pending — claim unbonded stake first
2.3 Propose Outcome

A registered resolver proposes YES or NO as the outcome for an expired market. This starts the dispute window.

  • Resolver must be registered with RRS ≥ 10 (Bronze minimum)
  • COI-1: Resolver must NOT hold any position in this market
  • COI-2: Resolver address must NOT be the market creator
  • Market must be STATUS_OPEN with current block > ExpiryTime
  • Proposal bond: max(1% of BEff, 60 PRX) deducted from stake
  • On success: market transitions to STATUS_PROPOSED
2.4 File Dispute

Anyone can file a dispute against a proposed outcome during the dispute window, escalating resolution to a panel of randomly selected resolvers (PORS).

  • Market must be STATUS_PROPOSED within the dispute window
  • Panel size: 5 resolvers (standard) or 7 (elevated risk — pool ≥ 25,000 PRX)
  • Panel selection uses RRS-weighted entropy from block hashes
2.5 Commit / Reveal / Tally Votes

Three-phase commit-reveal voting for panel resolvers. Prevents vote copying.

  • Commit Vote: panel member submits hash(vote ‖ salt) during commit window
  • Reveal Vote: panel member reveals vote and salt — hash must match
  • Tally Votes: anyone calls after reveal window — majority vote wins
  • Dissenting resolvers face an RRS penalty
  • Only panel members selected during file_dispute can vote
2.7 / 2.8 Unstake / Claim Unbonded

Resolvers can withdraw staked PRX. Unstaked tokens enter a 120,960-block (~7 day) unbonding period before they can be claimed.

  • Cannot unstake if UnbondingAmount > 0 already pending
  • Cannot unstake if resolver has an active open proposal (slash-evasion guard)
  • Partial unstake: remaining stake must be ≥ 500,000 PRX after unstake
  • Partial unstake RRS penalty: −10 points (floor: 0)
  • Full exit: RRS reset to 10, IsActive set to false
  • Claim Unbonded: call after UnbondingReleaseHeight to receive tokens
03

Admin

4.1 Create Market

Creates a new binary prediction market. The creator funds the initial LMSR liquidity (B0), pays a creator bond, and sets the expiry block.

Creating a market costs: B0 (≥ 60 PRX) + 5,000 PRX creator bond + 10,000 uPRX TX fee. The bond is returned on finalization.
  • B0 must be ≥ MIN_B0 (60 PRX)
  • ExpiryTime must be > current block height
  • Nonce must be unique per creator — used to derive deterministic MarketId
  • MarketId = SHA-256(CreatorAddress ‖ Nonce)[:20]
  • Market spam cap: max 50 open markets simultaneously per creator
  • 50 PRX finalization bounty locked — paid to whoever calls finalize_market
4.2 Finalize Market

Permissionless TX — any address can finalize a market after the dispute window closes. The caller receives the 50 PRX finalization bounty.

  • Market must be STATUS_PROPOSED with dispute window elapsed
  • On success: STATUS_FINALIZED, winning outcome written, winnings claimable
  • Caller receives 50 PRX FINALIZATION_BOUNTY
  • Creator bond (5,000 PRX) returned to creator
  • Resolver proposal bond returned, RRS +10 awarded
04

Conflict-of-Interest Guards

Praxis enforces three protocol-level COI guards to prevent market manipulation. All guards are enforced in DeliverTx (authoritative) and some additionally in CheckTx (early rejection).

COI-1 · CRITICAL
Resolver Position Ban
A resolver holding any YES or NO shares in a market cannot propose its outcome. Even 1 share is disqualifying.

Error code: 4001
COI-2 · HIGH
Creator/Resolver Overlap
The market creator cannot be the resolver for the same market. Enforced via address comparison in propose_outcome.

Error code: 4002
COI-3 · MEDIUM
Position Cap (20%)
A single address cannot hold more than 20% of the total pool value. Prevents wash trading and accumulation.

Error code: 4003

TX Fee Structure

All fee-collecting transactions split the TX fee 50/50 between the market fee pool and the global treasury pool. The treasury accumulates until the epoch boundary.
